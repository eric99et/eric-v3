A few more questions

Before you decide.

€129 for a lock?

No keys to lose or copy. Unlock with your phone, lend your bike with a link, and charge it twice a year. One lock that stays on your bike, with no subscription.

What if my phone dies?

Anyone you have shared the lock with can open it from their phone. Lending works with a link, so a partner or friend can hold a key for you.

What if the lock’s battery runs low?

About six months per charge, and the app warns you well before. A full charge over USB-C takes 90 minutes.

Can LAAS see where my bike is?

No. Your bike’s location is in Apple Find My. LAAS does not receive it.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. Use the LAAS app to unlock and share your bike. Apple Find My requires an Apple device.

Will my insurance accept it?

In Denmark, the Ring-Lock is insurance-approved: Varefakta VK 37868. Most Danish insurers ask for a Varefakta-approved lock and a lock certificate, and the certificate is in the box. Check your own policy. Outside Denmark, check what your policy asks for first.

How fast does it ship, and what does it cost?

It ships in 1–2 working days. Shipping for one lock is €9, and free from two locks. Questions about an order? Write to support@laaslocks.com.

How do returns and the right to complain work?

Changed your mind? Tell us at support@laaslocks.com within 57 days of delivery, then hand the lock to the carrier with the prepaid return label. You also have the two-year right to complain (reklamationsret) under Danish law, counted from delivery.
