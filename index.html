Will it fit my bike?

06 FAQ

Questions, answered.

Question not here? Call us on +45 40 85 30 99.

Will my insurance accept it?

In Denmark, the Ring-Lock is insurance-approved: Varefakta VK 37868. Most Danish insurers ask for a Varefakta-approved lock and a lock certificate, and the certificate is in the box. Check your own policy. Outside Denmark, check what your policy asks for first.

Will it fit my bike?

Most bikes, cargo bikes and e-bikes too: tyres up to 60 mm, mount spacing 75 to 100 mm. Not fat bikes. Frame holes? The mounting screws are in the box. No holes? The Flex Mount, €9.99.

What if the battery runs low?

About six months per charge. A full charge over USB-C takes 90 minutes, and any USB-C cable works.

Does it work with Android?

Yes. The LAAS app is on the App Store and Google Play.

How secure is it?

9 mm of hardened steel. Every unlock uses a one-time key over Bluetooth, so a recorded signal cannot be replayed.

How fast does it ship, and what does it cost?

It ships in 1–2 working days. Shipping is €9, and free on orders over €250. Questions about an order? Write to support@laaslocks.com.

How do returns and the right to complain work?

Changed your mind? Tell us at support@laaslocks.com within 57 days of delivery, then hand the lock to the carrier with the prepaid return label. You get your money back, shipping included, within 14 days of telling us, once the lock is back with us. You also have the two-year right to complain (reklamationsret) under Danish law, counted from delivery. Terms of sale
