04 Apple Find My

Works with Apple Find My.

After setup, you can locate your Ring-Lock in the Apple Find My app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. The location is end-to-end encrypted: neither LAAS nor Apple can see it.

To use the Apple Find My app to locate this item, the latest version of iOS, iPadOS, or macOS is recommended.

10 FAQ

Ask away.

We’re always happy to speak to you, whatever your question. Call +45 31 33 82 13 or write to support@laaslocks.com.

Which bikes does it fit?

Most city, commuter, hybrid, electric, cargo and family bikes. It is not designed for fat bikes. No mounting holes? Use Flex Mount, an optional accessory for frames without ring-lock mounting holes.

How safe is Ring-Lock?

Ring-Lock has passed Denmark’s strict Varefakta insurance tests. It is insurance approved in Denmark, and the insurance certificate is in the box.

How often does it need charging?

Roughly every six months. Lift the rubber cover and plug a USB-C cable into the lock. It can stay on the bike while charging.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. Use LAAS on Android to unlock and share your bike. The Android app is available on Google Play.

What if my phone is lost or replaced?

Signed in with Apple or Google? Install the LAAS app on another phone and sign in with the same account. Another Owner has access? Have them invite your new phone. Neither of those works? Call us on +45 31 33 82 13.

What is the Keep You Moving promise?

If our lock is the problem, it’s our job to fix it. We cover a shared bike, public transport or another reasonable option, up to €30. For two years from purchase. Full terms

When does it ship, and what does it cost?

It is a pre-order and ships in October. Shipping is €9, and free on orders over €250. Questions about an order? Write to support@laaslocks.com.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. You have 57 days to fall in love with it. If you’re not obsessed by then, you’ll get your money back. Tell us at support@laaslocks.com within 57 days of delivery, then send the lock back to us. You get your money back, shipping included, within 14 days of telling us, once the lock is back with us. You also have the two-year right to complain (reklamationsret) under Danish law, counted from delivery. Terms of sale
