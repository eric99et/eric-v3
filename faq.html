Fit & mounting

Will it fit my bike?

Most bikes do, cargo bikes and e-bikes too: tyres up to 60 mm wide and mount spacing 75 to 100 mm. It does not fit fat bikes. Not sure? Try it on your own bike: you have 57 days to send it back.

How do I mount it?

Most city bikes have two small threaded holes on the frame, either side of the rear wheel, made for a frame lock. Fix the Ring-Lock there with the two M5 screws from the box. It takes a few minutes.

My frame has no holes. What now?

Add the Flex Mount (€9.99). It holds the lock on a frame without holes, with no drilling. You can add it when you buy the lock or in the basket.

How much does it weigh?

About 504 g. It stays on the bike, so there is nothing to carry.

The app & unlocking

How do I open it?

One tap in the LAAS app and the 9 mm hardened-steel bolt slides open. To lock, push the bolt in until it clicks.

How do I connect the lock to the app?

Download the LAAS app from the App Store or Google Play, open it and follow the steps to add your lock.

Does it work with Android?

Yes. The LAAS app is on the App Store and Google Play. Apple Find My needs an Apple device, like an iPhone, iPad or Mac.

What if it will not open?

Check that nothing is in the way, like a spoke, and that the battery is charged. Still stuck? Call us on +45 40 85 30 99. If we cannot fix it over the phone, Keep You Moving pays up to €30 to get you where you are going.

Apple Find My & privacy

How do I add it to Apple Find My?

Open the Apple Find My app on your iPhone, add a new item and follow the steps. After setup, you can locate your Ring-Lock in the Apple Find My app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Is it live GPS tracking?

No. The Apple Find My network finds the lock through Apple devices that pass nearby, and shows it on the map. In a city that is usually close; where few people pass, it can take longer.

Who can see where my bike is?

Only you. The Apple Find My network uses advanced encryption, so no one else, not even Apple or LAAS, can view the location of your Ring-Lock.

Which iPhone do I need?

To use the Apple Find My app to locate this item, the latest version of iOS, iPadOS, or macOS is recommended.

Who is LAAS?

LAAS ApS is a Danish company in Copenhagen, CVR 40942491. We follow Danish and EU data protection law; see our privacy policy.

Battery & weather

How long does the battery last?

About six months per charge. A full charge over USB-C takes about 90 minutes, with any USB-C charger.

Does it handle rain and winter?

Yes. It is IP55: made for rain, road spray and a Danish winter.

Theft & insurance

Is it approved for Danish insurance?

Yes. The Ring-Lock is on Varefakta’s public list of approved locks (VK 37868, certificate VF 5029:4). Most Danish insurers ask for an approved lock, and this one meets that requirement.

What is the lock certificate?

The paper your insurer asks for if your bike is stolen. It is in the box. Keep it somewhere safe and send it with the claim.

Can it be cut?

With an angle grinder, yes, like every bike lock on the market. What a grinder cannot do is work quietly or quickly, and Apple Find My can still help you find the bike afterwards.

My bike is gone. What now?

Open the Apple Find My app first: many “stolen” bikes have only been moved. If it really is stolen, report it to the police and send the lock certificate to your insurer with the claim.

Pasform og montering

Passer den til min cykel?

De fleste cykler, også ladcykler og elcykler: dæk op til 60 mm brede og en monteringsafstand på 75 til 100 mm. Den passer ikke til fatbikes. I tvivl? Prøv den på din egen cykel: du har 57 dage til at sende den retur.

Hvordan monterer jeg den?

De fleste bycykler har to små gevindhuller i stellet, på hver side af baghjulet, lavet til en ringlås. Spænd Ring-Lock fast der med de to M5-skruer fra æsken. Det tager få minutter.

Mit stel har ingen huller. Hvad nu?

Tilføj Flex Mount (75 kr.). Den holder låsen på et stel uden huller, uden at bore. Du kan tilføje den, når du køber låsen, eller i kurven.

Hvor meget vejer den?

Cirka 504 g. Den bliver på cyklen, så der er intet at bære rundt på.

Apple Find My og privatliv

Hvordan tilføjer jeg den til Apple Find My?

Åbn Apple Find My-appen på din iPhone, tilføj en ny genstand og følg trinnene. Når den er sat op, kan du finde din Ring-Lock i Apple Find My-appen på din iPhone, iPad eller Mac.

Er det live GPS-sporing?

Nej. Apple Find My-netværket finder låsen via Apple-enheder, der kommer forbi, og viser den på kortet. I en by er det som regel tæt på; hvor få kommer forbi, kan det tage længere tid.

Hvem kan se, hvor min cykel er?

Kun dig. Apple Find My-netværket bruger avanceret kryptering, så ingen andre, heller ikke Apple eller LAAS, kan se, hvor din Ring-Lock er.

Hvilken iPhone skal jeg bruge?

For at bruge Apple Find My-appen til at finde denne genstand anbefales den nyeste version af iOS, iPadOS eller macOS.

Tyveri og forsikring

Er den godkendt til danske forsikringer?

Ja. Ring-Lock står på Varefaktas offentlige liste over godkendte låse (VK 37868, certifikat VF 5029:4). De fleste danske forsikringer kræver en godkendt lås, og denne lever op til kravet.

Hvad er låseattesten?

Det papir, dit forsikringsselskab beder om, hvis din cykel bliver stjålet. Den ligger i æsken. Gem den et sikkert sted og send den med anmeldelsen.

Kan den skæres over?

Med en vinkelsliber, ja, som alle cykellåse på markedet. Men en vinkelsliber arbejder hverken stille eller hurtigt, og Apple Find My kan stadig hjælpe dig med at finde cyklen bagefter.

Min cykel er væk. Hvad nu?

Åbn Apple Find My-appen først: mange “stjålne” cykler er bare blevet flyttet. Er den virkelig stjålet, så anmeld det til politiet og send låseattesten til dit forsikringsselskab med anmeldelsen.
